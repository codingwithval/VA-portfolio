About

Focus on what matters.

A virtual assistant is like a third arm that takes care of the non-essential routine administrative and executive work so you can focus on building your business and directing your attention to your clients and where the money is.

I know it sounds jarring to leave your business in the hands of someone you don’t get to meet and see all the time, but if you can build trust and communicate your requirements clearly, you’ll find that it’s almost as easy as having a front-desk assistant. This is where I come in.

I’m Val, your dedicated and reliable VA. Over the last 5 years, I have worked with over 12 diverse clients in the Americas, Europe, Australia, and Africa in dynamic environments where speed, reliability, and accuracy were what mattered the most. With a bachelor’s degree in project management and experience working with teams in different industries (digital marketing, health, insurance, law, and specialty trade contractors), I know how to balance the given scope, time, and resources so you can reach your goals with minimum wastage.
